---
title: Supported and Refused
description: What formualizer recalc recalculates (dynamic spills, CSE arrays, tables, volatile functions) and what it refuses (data tables, external links, hidden-row SUBTOTAL, shifted-sheet shared formulas, ...), with the meaning of each refusal message.
---

`formualizer recalc` uses a strict source-preserving path. It recalculates a defined subset of XLSX and refuses everything else as a whole: a refused workbook exits 2, nothing is written, and its caches must not be reported as recalculated. There is no fallback engine. Formula errors such as `#DIV/0!` or `#SPILL!` are different: they are calculated results, reported in `errors` with exit 0.

A refusal in `--json` output looks like this:

```json
{"schema":"formualizer.recalc/1","status":"refused","input":"model.xlsx","output":"model.xlsx","written":false,"formula_cells":null,"cache_cells_changed":null,"worksheet_parts_changed":null,"error_cells":null,"errors":null,"errors_truncated":null,"unknown_functions":null,"refusal":{"feature":"data-table formula","context":"worksheet"},"clock":null,"seed":null,"message":"model.xlsx: Unsupported feature: data-table formula in worksheet. Nothing was written."}
```
